## Themes:

- Theme: [MonoThemeDark](https://github.com/witalihirsch/Mono-gtk-theme/) , [Download](https://github.com/witalihirsch/Mono-gtk-theme/releases/) , [Installation](https://github.com/witalihirsch/Mono-gtk-theme#installing)

- Icons: [Yet-Another-Monochrome-Icon-Set(YAMIS)](https://github.com/googIyEYES/YAMIS) , [Download and Installation](https://github.com/googIyEYES/YAMIS#installation)

- Cursor: [volantes-cursors](https://github.com/varlesh/volantes-cursors) , [Download](https://www.opendesktop.org/p/1356095/) , Installation (dwl config file. `lxappearance`. `nwg-look`)

- Fonts: [Vazirmatn](https://github.com/rastikerdar/vazirmatn) , [JetBrainsMono Nerd Font](https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts)
